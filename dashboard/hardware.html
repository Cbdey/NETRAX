Live sensors

Trend lines show the last 5 minutes. Dashed red = alarm threshold, dotted grey = learned baseline. MQ and FSR values are raw ADC counts (0–4095), not ppm. Missing readings show “—”, never as safe.

Fire & smoke cross-check

A camera fire or smoke detection waits for the safety station of that zone. Flame sensor, MQ-2 or temperature confirms → CRITICAL and siren. Station stays green → not alarmed (camera only). Station offline → camera alarm goes ahead.
