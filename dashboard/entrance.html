How verification works

  1. 1
    Tap the RFID card or look at the camera
    Registered face or card = the worker's name. Neither = unregistered worker.
  2. 2
    Stand in front of the camera for about 2 s
    Whole body in the picture, head to shoes, one person at a time.
  3. 3
    Green = enter, red = do not enter
    Unclear picture or a card that does not match the face = manual check, never automatic approval.
